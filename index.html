Ludo

Race your four tokens round the board and home. 2 to 4 players.

You need to be signed in with start.ng. Make a room, send the code to up to 3 friends, add bots to fill empty seats, and start. The server rolls the die and checks every move.

    How to play

    Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Move one token the number you rolled, round the ring and up your own home squares; you need the exact number to finish. A 6 gives you another roll (three 6s in a row lose the turn). Landing on someone else's token sends it back to its base and gives you another roll. Squares with a star or a coloured start square are safe. First to get all four tokens home wins.
